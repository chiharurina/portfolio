Block system, live

A conceptual simulation of the block logic I'm designing as Controls Lead for our ride engineering competition coaster. A train may only leave its block when the next one is proven clear.

How to use: the trains run on their own. Tap a block to cut its sensor power, or use the operator panel to stop the ride. Reset simulation puts everything back.

Event log
    • Cut a sensor. Tap any block. With power lost it reads occupied, so the train behind it holds. That's the fail-safe default.
    • Hit E-STOP mid-course. The lift stops at once, but a coasting train can't. It runs to the next brake and holds there.
    • Run it yourself. Turn off Auto, then lock restraints and close gates before Dispatch will arm.

    Educational simulation only. It illustrates block and interlock concepts with simplified train motion. It is not the competition ride's control code and does not validate the safety of any real ride.
